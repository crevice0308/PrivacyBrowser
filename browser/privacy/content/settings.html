Zero History

All history categories start off. Bookmarks and downloaded files are kept.

Restart after changing privacy settings.

Zero History controls browser-managed data. Operating system logs, DNS caches, swap, crash dumps, and traces in websites or network services are outside its scope. Data that was already saved stays until you clear it. Downloaded files and bookmarks are preserved.

Closed-tab recovery follows session recovery settings. Turning a category off can remove that category’s previously saved data during privacy cleanup.

Search

A separate space for questions. The address bar stays for URLs.

Include {searchTerms} exactly once where the query belongs.

Performance & shutdown

Keep background work light, and close the browser when you’re done.

Firefox’s sandbox, site isolation, certificate checks, and download protection remain enabled. Manage site permissions in the page’s address-bar permission controls or Firefox-compatible settings.

Diagnostics

Optional local logs to help investigate a problem.

Save locationBrowser profile diagnostics folder

Diagnostic events use a restricted schema. URLs, searches, cookies, passwords, and form values are excluded. Packages are saved locally for you to inspect and share.
